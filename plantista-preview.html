Plant care tips

01

Check soil before watering

Push a finger 2 inches into the soil. Water only if it comes out dry.

02

Rotate for even growth

Turn pots a quarter turn every week so plants grow evenly toward light.

03

Wipe leaves monthly

Dust blocks light absorption. A damp cloth keeps leaves photosynthesizing well.
